Notary Basics

Why Do Documents Need to Be Notarized?

Notarization is designed to deter fraud by verifying the identity of the signer, confirming personal appearance, and documenting the notarial act. A notary does not determine whether a document is legally sufficient or provide legal advice.

Notary Basics

What Is a Jurat?

A jurat generally involves the signer swearing or affirming that the contents of a document are true and signing in the notary's presence. It differs from an acknowledgment, in which the signer acknowledges a signature and voluntary execution.

Employment Verification

I-9 Authorized Representative Service: Not a Notarization

An employer may designate an authorized representative to complete the employer review portion of Form I-9. When a notary serves in that capacity, the notary is acting as the employer's authorized representative, not performing a notarial act, and does not apply a notary seal to Form I-9.
