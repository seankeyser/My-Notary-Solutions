How Does Remote Online Notarization Work? A Step-by-Step Guide

Remote Online Notarization—often shortened to RON—allows an eligible notarial act to be completed electronically while the signer and online notary communicate in real time using audio-video technology. For many signers, that means completing a notarization without traveling to a notary office or arranging an in-person meeting.

Sean M. Keyser Notary Solutions provides Remote Online Notary services under Florida law while Sean is physically located in Florida. A signer may be located outside Florida, subject to the document, transaction, and receiving-party requirements.

Quick Answer

What happens during an online notarization?

You request a session, prepare the document and identification, complete the required identity verification, meet the online notary through live audio-video communication, and electronically complete the eligible document and notarial certificate.

Step 1: Request Your Remote Online Notary Session

Start by providing basic information about the transaction. Helpful details include the type of document, number of signers, your city and state, whether witnesses are required, and your preferred appointment time.

This first step also provides an opportunity to identify whether the document appears appropriate for Remote Online Notarization. If the document came from an attorney, title company, lender, financial institution, court, government agency, or another organization, confirm any special execution or acceptance requirements with that organization.

Step 2: Prepare Your Document, Device, and Identification

Before the session, have the items needed to participate successfully. These commonly include:

  • A reliable internet connection
  • A compatible computer, tablet, or other supported device
  • A working camera and microphone
  • An acceptable government-issued identification credential
  • The electronic document requiring notarization
  • Any additional signers or witnesses required for the transaction
  • Access to your email and the RON platform instructions

Do not sign a document in advance when the particular notarial act requires the signature to be made in the notary's presence. If you are unsure about when to sign, wait for instructions during the session.

Step 3: Complete the Required Identity Verification

Identity verification is one of the important differences between a compliant Remote Online Notarization and an ordinary video call. When a remotely appearing principal is not personally known to the Florida online notary, applicable RON procedures include remote presentation of identification, credential analysis, and identity proofing.

The secure RON platform guides the signer through the required steps before or as part of the online appointment.

Step 4: Meet the Notary Through Live Audio-Video Communication

During the scheduled session, the signer and online notary communicate in real time through two-way audio-video technology. The notary must be able to see and hear the remotely appearing participant as required for the notarial act.

The notary confirms the signer's identity and handles the notarial procedure appropriate to the document and the notarial act selected by the signer or provided by the document's issuing or receiving party.

Step 5: Electronically Sign and Complete the Notarial Act

For an eligible electronic transaction, the required signatures and electronic notarial certificate are completed through the online platform. The exact procedure can vary depending on the document and the type of notarial act.

Remote Online Notarization does not change the notary's role. A notary verifies identity and performs the requested notarial act but does not determine whether a document is legally sufficient or advise a signer about which legal document to use.

Step 6: Receive the Completed Electronic Document

After the session is successfully completed, the electronically notarized document can be handled according to the platform and transaction instructions. Before relying on RON for a particular transaction, make sure the organization receiving the document accepts the electronic format and Remote Online Notarization.

Important: Not every document or receiving organization accepts Remote Online Notarization. When acceptance is uncertain, check with the organization that will receive or record the document before scheduling.

Can I Use a Florida Remote Online Notary From Another State?

A signer does not necessarily have to be physically located in Florida. Florida law allows a Florida online notary who is physically located in Florida to perform an online notarization when a principal or witness is located outside Florida, provided the applicable requirements are met. The notarial act itself is performed under Florida law.

This can make RON useful for travelers, family members in different states, business transactions involving participants in different locations, and other situations where an in-person meeting would be inconvenient.

What Types of Documents May Be Notarized Online?

Depending on the document and receiving-party requirements, examples may include:

  • Affidavits and sworn statements
  • Powers of Attorney
  • Contracts and agreements
  • Insurance and banking forms
  • Settlement documents
  • Trust and estate documents
  • Some real estate and mortgage documents
  • Other eligible personal, business, and financial documents

Is Remote Online Notarization Right for Me?

RON may be especially convenient when you are unable to travel, are located away from the notary, have participants in different locations, or simply prefer to handle an eligible notarization online.
