FAQ

Remote Online Notary questions.

Can I use Remote Online Notarization if I am not in Florida?

Yes, a Florida online notary who is physically located in Florida may perform a compliant online notarization even when a principal or witness is outside Florida. Document and recipient requirements still apply.

How much does it cost?

$25 for the first notarial act and $15 for each additional act completed during the same session.

Can every document be notarized online?

No. Eligibility depends on the document, applicable requirements, and acceptance by the receiving organization.

Is identity verification required?

Yes. Florida law specifies identity-verification procedures for remotely appearing principals when they are not personally known to the online notary.
