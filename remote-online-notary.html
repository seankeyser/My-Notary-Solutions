What is Remote Online Notarization?

Remote Online Notarization, commonly called RON, is a method of completing a notarial act electronically while the signer and notary communicate in real time using approved audio-video technology.

Instead of meeting a notary face-to-face at the same physical location, the signer completes identity-verification requirements and appears before the online notary during a live audio-video session.

For clients, the primary advantage is convenience. An eligible document can often be handled without arranging travel or coordinating an in-person appointment.

Remote Online Notary FAQ

Questions about notarizing documents online.

Can I use Remote Online Notarization if I am not in Florida?

Yes. A Florida online notary who is physically located in Florida may perform a compliant online notarization even when the principal or witnesses are outside Florida. The document and receiving party must still permit or accept the transaction.

How much does Remote Online Notarization cost?

Sean M. Keyser Notary Solutions charges $25 for the first online notarial act and $15 for each additional notarial act completed during the same session.

What do I need for an online notarization?

You generally need a reliable internet connection, a compatible device with a camera and microphone, an acceptable government-issued identification credential, your document, and any additional signers or witnesses required for the transaction.

Can every document be notarized online?

No. Whether RON is appropriate can depend on the document, applicable requirements, and whether the receiving organization accepts an electronically notarized document.

How is my identity verified during a Florida RON session?

When the principal is not personally known to the online notary, Florida law provides for remote presentation of a government-issued identification credential, credential analysis, and identity proofing that meets applicable requirements.

Is Remote Online Notarization the same as a video call with a notary?

No. A compliant Florida online notarization involves more than an ordinary video call. The transaction must use audio-video communication technology and processes that satisfy applicable Florida Remote Online Notarization requirements.

Can witnesses participate remotely?

Florida law provides procedures for an online notary to supervise the witnessing of certain electronic records, including situations where a witness participates remotely. Whether that procedure is appropriate depends on the document and transaction requirements.
