Why Mobile Notary?

Convenient service at your location.

The Villages is a large community, and driving to a notary office isn't always convenient. A mobile notary appointment brings professional notary service directly to your home, villa, or another agreed location — saving you time and effort.

Whether you need a power of attorney notarized, a living will witnessed, or estate documents handled, mobile notary service means you can complete the appointment comfortably at home.

If you prefer not to meet in person, Remote Online Notarization is also available for eligible documents.

What to Have Ready

Preparing for your appointment.

  • Valid government-issued photo ID for each signer
  • The document to be notarized (unsigned unless instructed otherwise)
  • Any additional signers required for the document
  • Any required witnesses, if applicable

Note: Do not sign the document before your appointment unless specifically instructed. Many notarial acts require the signature to be made in the notary's presence.
